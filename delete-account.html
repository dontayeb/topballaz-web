Delete your TopBallaz account

You can delete your account from inside the app, or ask us to delete it for you.

From the app

  1. Open TopBallaz and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Under Delete account, tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. There is no waiting period and no way to undo it.

If you cannot open the app

Email zaristudio25@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or tell us the phone number you signed up with, and ask for your account to be deleted. We will confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details — email address and phone number.
  • Your profile, including your real name if you entered one, and your photo.
  • Your privacy settings and any device preferences held against your account.

What stays, and why

  • Your name on past results. Goals you scored stay credited to your name in your group's match history and season table, as an unclaimed name. Removing them would change the group's own records and the results they have already shared with each other. The name is kept with nothing attached to it — no email, no phone number, no way to contact you or sign in as you.
  • Games you run. If you own a game, it passes to the next admin, or to the longest-standing member if there is no other admin. A game with nobody else in it is deleted along with your account.
  • Audit entries. Records of changes made to a game are kept for the people still in it, with your name removed from them.

If you want your name removed from past results as well, say so in your email and we will replace it with a placeholder.

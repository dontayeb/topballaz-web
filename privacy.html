Privacy policy

TopBallaz · last updated 7 October 2026

TopBallaz is an app for running a recreational football group: fixtures, who is playing, results and a season table. This policy describes what it collects, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to cover us.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Email address or phone numberTo sign you in. One or the other, depending on how you choose to sign in.
Your Google account's email and name, if you sign in with GoogleTo sign you in. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or anything else.
Display name (your nickname)How your group sees you on the roster, in results and in the season table.
Real name, optionalOnly so an admin can match you to a name on an old roster. It is never shown to anyone, on any screen.
Goals, match results, RSVPs and check-insThe point of the app. This is your group's record of its own games.
A record of admin changesSo a group can see who changed a result and why.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.
  • No location. The app does not request or record your location.
  • No contacts, photos or files beyond anything you deliberately add.
  • No advertising identifiers, and nothing is shared with advertisers.

The app asks for camera access only to scan a QR code when joining a game or checking in at the pitch. Nothing from the camera is stored, uploaded or seen by us.

Your choice of 12 or 24-hour time is kept on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Who can see what

  • Members of a game can see each other's display names, goals, attendance and RSVPs for that game.
  • Your real name is never shown to anyone, including admins and the people who run your group.
  • Your email address and phone number are never shown to other members.
  • A game can be set to public by whoever runs it, which means its name and results may be visible outside the group. Your display name and goals would be visible as part of that. Games are private unless someone chooses otherwise.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for marketing.

Where it is kept

Data is stored with Supabase, who host the database and handle sign-in on our behalf, on servers in Canada. Supabase processes it only to provide that service.

If you sign in with Google, Google handles that sign-in under their own privacy policy.

These are the only third parties involved. There are no others.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete account, or by emailing us. It happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Your account, email address, phone number, real name and photo are deleted. Your display name stays on past results and the season table as an unclaimed name, so your group's own records and the results they have already shared do not change — with nothing attached to it, no way to contact you and no way to sign in as you. Ask us and we will replace that name with a placeholder too.

Full details, and how to ask us to do it for you.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email zaristudio25@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can change your display name and real name yourself at any time from the Profile tab.

Children

TopBallaz is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who can see it, the date at the top changes and the app will tell you the next time you open it.
